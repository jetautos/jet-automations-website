Getting Started: Your FAA Medical Certificate

Before you can fly solo, the FAA requires a medical certificate. For most private pilot students, you'll need at least a Third-Class Medical.

StepWhat to doOfficial resource
1Determine which certificate you need (Third-Class for PPL)FAA Medical Standards
2Complete MedXPress online (~20–30 min)MedXPress
3Find an AME and scheduleAME Locator
4Bring ID, glasses/contacts if applicable, MedXPress confirmation—
5Complete exam; receive certificate same day if qualified—

MedXPress tips: Answer every question honestly. List all medications and visits. Save your confirmation number.

Finding an AME near Pleasanton / San Antonio: Search the FAA AME Locator using zip codes 78064 (Pleasanton), 782 (San Antonio), or 781 (Floresville area). Cost is typically $100–$150 out of pocket.

Eagle Aviation tip: You can start ground lessons and fly with an instructor before your medical is finalized — but get the process started early. Contact us with questions.

Applying for Your Student Pilot Certificate

Since 2016, most applicants use IACRA — no more trips to the FSDO for a paper certificate.

  1. Create an IACRA account at iacra.faa.gov
  2. Your CFI helps you apply during early lessons
  3. Select: Pilot → Student Pilot Certificate
  4. Submit and wait for processing (often 1–3 weeks)
  5. CFI endorsement allows solo flight when you're ready

Official guide: FAA Student Pilot Certificate

Flying in South Texas

  • •Summer heat: Schedule dawn or dusk lessons when density altitude is lower.
  • •Gulf moisture: Haze is normal — you'll learn visibility judgment.
  • •Afternoon convection: Spring/summer pop-up storms — flexibility is a pilot superpower.
  • •Crosswinds: South Texas breezes build real crosswind skills early.
  • •Wide open airspace: Less congested than metro airports — great for building hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a medical before my discovery flight?

No. You can experience your first lesson without a medical. You'll need one before solo flight, so start the process early.

How old do I need to be?

You can start training at any age, but you must be 16 for solo and 17 for a Private Pilot Certificate.

How far is Eagle Aviation from San Antonio?

About 35–40 minutes south via US-281.

What aircraft do you train in?

A Cessna 150 — see our fleet.

Part 61 or Part 141?

Eagle Aviation trains under Part 61 — flexible scheduling ideal for working adults.

How long does it take to get a private pilot license?

Most dedicated students finish in 6–12 months.

Does the discovery flight count as a real lesson?

Yes. ~1 hour, gas included, and the $195 applies toward training when you enroll.

Do you offer flight reviews?

Yes — see our flight reviews page.
